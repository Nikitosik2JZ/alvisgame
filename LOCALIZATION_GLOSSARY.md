Restaurant brands Burger House, Pizza Point and Sushi Place are intentional proper names in both languages. WASD, E, arrows, ₽ and mathematical symbols remain universal. Yandex ID, VIP and HUD are intentional abbreviations. Generated employee names have paired Cyrillic/transliterated spellings; custom company names and platform player names are user data and remain intact.

Translation keys are stable, grouped by their original system (for example `order-ui`, `events`, `company-events`, `achievements`, `daily-tasks`). Whole sentence templates use `{{variable}}`, including their punctuation and word order. New strings must be added to both catalogs; `npm run build` rejects missing keys, mismatched parameters and suspicious hardcoded UI text.
